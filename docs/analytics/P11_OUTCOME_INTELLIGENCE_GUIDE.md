# P11 Outcome Intelligence Guide

## Business Value vs. Activity
Activity (how many tasks) does not equal Value (how much was achieved).

## Key Metrics
- **Time Saved**: Human equivalent hours saved by autonomous agents.
- **Cost Reduction**: Direct reduction in operational expenses.
- **Risk Suppression**: Number of incidents prevented by the `SafetyKernel`.

## ROI Estimation
The `ROIEstimator` provides a dollar-value estimate of the system's contribution to the organization.
Generate reports using `scripts/outcome_export_report.py`.
