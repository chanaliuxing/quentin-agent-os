# P12 Stewardship Council Guide

## Goal
To manage the long-term evolution of the OQS and Quentin OS core in a decentralized manner.

## Council Protocol
- **Proposals**: Any High-Trust node can submit an OQS evolution proposal.
- **Voting**: Member nodes vote `approve` or `reject`.
- **Threshold**: Proposals require a simple majority (>50%) to pass.

## Participation
Join the council by registering your node with a `trust_level="high"` and meeting the certification criteria.
